1.Our service

We provide private, pre-arranged ground transportation by chauffeured vehicle. Service is by reservation only — we are not a taxi and do not accept street hails. All rides are private: your vehicle is not shared with other parties.

Reservations are for one vehicle. The vehicle seats up to four passengers with standard luggage. If your party or luggage exceeds that capacity, tell us before booking so we can confirm we can carry you safely.

2.Rates and what is included

Airport rates are flat rates per vehicle, one way — not per person. The rate quoted when you book is the rate you pay, regardless of traffic or the route taken.

Included in the flat rate: the vehicle, your chauffeur, and fuel. Nothing else is built into the price.

Gratuity is optional and never included in the rate. When you book you are offered a gratuity of 15%, 18%, 20%, 22.5%, 25% or 30%, or you may enter your own amount or decline entirely. Whatever you choose is shown as its own line before you confirm, and is added to the fare to give the total you are charged. You may also add a gratuity after your ride.

Billed separately, at cost, after the ride: tolls and airport parking. Florida Turnpike tolls on the Orlando and Miami routes are significant and are passed through exactly as incurred, with no markup.

3.Payment authorization and when your card is charged

Your card is charged in full when you book. The total shown before you confirm — the flat rate plus any gratuity you chose — is taken immediately, and your ride is reserved at that moment. If you cancel more than 24 hours before pickup, you are refunded in full.

When you book, you provide a payment card and authorize us to store it and charge it as described here. The card is stored securely by our payment processor, Authorize.net. We never see, handle, or store your full card number — we hold only a reference token that lets us charge that card and nothing else.

What you are authorizing

  • The total shown at checkout — the flat rate plus any gratuity you selected — charged at the moment you confirm the booking.
  • Additional charges after your ride for wait time, tolls, airport parking, extra stops, or cleaning, as described in sections 4 and 5. These are itemized and receipted.
  • Cancellation charges, if you cancel inside the windows described in section 6.

You will receive an emailed receipt for every charge. Your authorization remains in effect for this reservation and any additional charges arising from it. To remove a stored card, or to ask any question about a charge, call or text 848-667-0999.

If your card is declined, the booking is not created and the time is released for someone else. Nothing is charged and no reservation exists — try another card, or call us and we will arrange it.

7.Delays, weather, and missed flights

We plan generous travel time and we track your flight. However, we cannot control traffic, road closures, weather, or airport conditions. We are not responsible for missed flights or connections, and we recommend allowing ample time for check-in and security.

If conditions make travel genuinely unsafe, we may cancel the ride. You are charged nothing in that case.

8.Conduct, safety, and refusal of service

  • Seat belts are required by Florida law and must be worn at all times.
  • Florida law requires children under six to be secured in a federally approved child restraint. Please request a child seat when booking.
  • Smoking and vaping are not permitted in the vehicle.
  • Passenger count may not exceed the vehicle's seating capacity for any reason.

We may decline or end a ride where a passenger is behaving unsafely, is abusive to the chauffeur, exceeds vehicle capacity, or where continuing would be unlawful. Where service is refused for these reasons, the fare is treated as a no-show under section 6.

9.Luggage and lost property

Please keep valuables, medication, and travel documents with you. We are not responsible for items left in the vehicle, though we will always make a genuine effort to return anything found. Unclaimed items are held for 30 days.

Luggage is limited to what the vehicle can carry safely. Oversized items — golf clubs, bicycles, large boxes — must be arranged before booking.
